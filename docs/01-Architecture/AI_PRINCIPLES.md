# TechHarvest AI Principles

## 1. Every AI employee has one responsibility.

Admissions sells.

Instructor teaches.

Finance handles money.

Support solves technical problems.

Marketing generates leads.

Student Success keeps students engaged.

---

## 2. Every AI employee shares one knowledge base.

No duplicated knowledge.

Everything comes from the Knowledge Engine.

---

## 3. Every AI employee communicates through the AI Orchestrator.

Agents never call one another directly.

---

## 4. Every AI action must be traceable.

Every conversation can be reviewed.

Every decision is logged.

---

## 5. Every external action uses tools.

Examples:

- Send WhatsApp
- Send Email
- Verify Payment
- Create Student
- Issue Certificate
- Create Invoice

AI never performs these directly.

It requests a tool.

The tool executes.
